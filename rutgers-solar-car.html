RUTGERS SOLAR CAR TEAM

The Rutgers Solar Car Team is a multidisciplinary, student-led organization at Rutgers University that designs, builds, and races custom solar-powered vehicles from scratch. The team puts its engineering to the test at two major national competitions: the Formula Sun Grand Prix (FSGP), a track endurance race preceded by rigorous technical inspections, and the American Solar Challenge (ASC), a 1,500+ mile cross-country road rally.

Hyperion is the team's single-occupant competition car for the 2026 season, brought to life by over 100 students across 10 disciplines. Designed and built in-house over several years, it was engineered from the ground up through student-led machining, composite layups, and electrical work. Hyperion set a major milestone at Brainerd International Raceway during FSGP 2026, becoming the first fully in-house Rutgers solar car to pass technical scrutineering, get on track, and log official competition laps.
